RepClimb

Privacy Policy

RepClimb is designed to keep your training data private and your experience focused.

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RepClimb handles information when you use the RepClimb iOS application. RepClimb does not sell personal information, display advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

RepClimb is operated by Raif Agovic, an independent developer, who is the data controller for personal information handled directly for RepClimb. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to raif.agovic.dev@gmail.com or submitted through the support page.

Workout Data

Your workout information, including exercises, sets, repetitions, progression levels, workout history, and recovery schedule, is stored locally on your device. RepClimb does not upload this workout data to servers operated by the developer.

Removing RepClimb from your device deletes this locally stored workout information, subject to the normal behavior of device backups managed by Apple.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. RepClimb does not receive or store your payment-card information.

RepClimb uses RevenueCat to provide purchase status, purchase validation, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, entitlement status, and limited app or device information required to operate these services. This information is used for app functionality, purchase analytics, and fraud prevention.

RevenueCat's handling of information is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, RepClimb processes information:

  • to perform a contract, including providing and restoring purchased access;
  • to take steps at your request, including responding to support and privacy inquiries;
  • for legitimate interests such as maintaining app security, preventing fraud, and improving support, where those interests are not overridden by your rights; and
  • to comply with legal obligations or with your consent where consent is required.

Support Communications

If you contact support, the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, subject, and message, is used only to respond to your request and resolve the issue. The support form is processed by Formspree, which delivers your submission to RepClimb support. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Formspree's handling of information is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

RepClimb does not sell or rent information. Information is shared only with service providers required to deliver app functionality and support, such as Apple, RevenueCat, and Formspree, or when required by law. RepClimb does not use personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

RepClimb requires service providers handling information on its behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. RevenueCat and Formspree may process information in the United States or other countries. Where required, international transfers are handled under the legal and contractual safeguards described in those providers' privacy documentation.

Retention and Deletion

Workout data remains on your device until you delete the app or otherwise remove the data. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or comply with law. Purchase and entitlement records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies and as needed to administer or restore purchases.

You may request deletion of support information or information controlled directly by RepClimb through the support page or email address below. Some information may be retained when required by law or necessary to document a transaction or resolve a dispute.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal information, and to request a portable copy. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

  • You can delete locally stored workout data by deleting the app.
  • You can restore your one-time purchase through Restore Purchases in RepClimb.
  • You can request help concerning support communications or RevenueCat purchase data through the RepClimb support page.

Security

RepClimb uses reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the limited information it handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

RepClimb is not directed to children below the minimum age at which they may provide personal information under applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through support, contact RepClimb so the information can be reviewed and deleted where required.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when RepClimb's features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the RepClimb support page or emailed to raif.agovic.dev@gmail.com.
