Privacy Policy

RepClimb is designed to keep your training data private and your experience focused.

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RepClimb handles information when you use the RepClimb iOS application. RepClimb does not sell personal information, display advertising, or track users across apps or websites.

Workout Data

Your workout information, including exercises, sets, repetitions, progression levels, workout history, and recovery schedule, is stored locally on your device. RepClimb does not upload this workout data to servers operated by the developer.

Removing RepClimb from your device deletes this locally stored workout information, subject to the normal behavior of device backups managed by Apple.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. RepClimb does not receive or store your payment-card information.

RepClimb uses RevenueCat to provide subscription status, purchase validation, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, entitlement status, and limited app or device information required to operate these services. This information is used for app functionality, subscription analytics, and fraud prevention.

RevenueCat's handling of information is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Support Communications

If you contact support, the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and message, is used only to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Data Sharing

RepClimb does not sell or rent information. Information is shared only with service providers required to deliver app functionality, such as Apple and RevenueCat, or when required by law. RepClimb does not use personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Your Choices

  • You can delete locally stored workout data by deleting the app.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account settings.
  • You can request help concerning support communications or RevenueCat subscription data through the RepClimb support page.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when RepClimb's features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the RepClimb support page.
