Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription from Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases inside RepClimb when an active subscription is not recognized.

Privacy Requests

Workout data is stored locally and can be removed by deleting RepClimb. For questions about support communications or subscription data processed through RevenueCat, contact support using the button above.
