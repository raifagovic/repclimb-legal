Support

Need help with RepClimb, your subscription, or a privacy request? Send a message and include enough detail for us to investigate.

Contact Support

Send us a message and we will reply to the email address you provide. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription from Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Use Restore Purchases inside RepClimb when an active subscription is not recognized.

Privacy Requests

Workout data is stored locally and can be removed by deleting RepClimb. For questions about support communications or subscription data processed through RevenueCat, use the form above.
